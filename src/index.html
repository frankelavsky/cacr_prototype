Child-Centered Governance in Action

Explore how communities across the country are putting child-centered governance strategies into practice.

Child-centered governance (CCG) gives local governments practical ways to consider children's needs and perspectives in decision-making, policies and public investments. The National League of Cities and UNICEF USA surveyed 384 municipalities nationwide to understand where and how communities are adopting and implementing child-centered governance practices.

    About this data

    The dashboard presents self-reported responses from 384 municipalities participating in a National League of Cities and UNICEF USA survey. Responses reflect practices municipalities reported as in practice, in planning or not currently active at the time of the survey. The findings represent participating municipalities and should not be interpreted as estimates for all U.S. municipalities. Survey responses have not necessarily been independently verified. Learn more about the survey methodology [here]

    This is self-reported data. If you see incorrect data or would like to update your city email research@unicefusa.org.
